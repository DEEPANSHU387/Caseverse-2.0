Ditto Insurance Strategy & Analytics Dashboard

> National Case Competition Project | CaseVerse 2.0 – Analytics & Consulting Club, NIT Rourkela

Project Overview

This project was developed as part of **CaseVerse 2.0**, a national-level case competition organized by the Analytics & Consulting Club, NIT Rourkela.

The challenge focused on creating a comprehensive growth strategy for **Ditto Insurance**, addressing the evolving Indian insurance market after the proposed increase in Foreign Direct Investment (FDI). The solution combines business strategy with interactive data analytics to support informed decision-making.

Problem Statement

Develop a strategic roadmap for Ditto Insurance by:

- Assessing the impact of increased competition in the insurance sector.
- Identifying growth opportunities.
- Positioning Ditto as a trusted insurance advisor.
- Proposing a technology and product development roadmap.
- Identifying strategic partnerships for long-term expansion.
